## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

The **Metric G: Scenario Capability View** integrates macroeconomic labor-market foresight from the frozen **M6 Scenario Engine v1.1.0** into workforce sustainability analytics.

Workforce transformation is non-linear; the urgency of acquiring sustainability and digital capabilities shifts significantly under different trajectories of artificial intelligence adoption. Metric G enables corporate planners and policy analysts to observe how occupational exposure and reskilling priorities evolve across three standardized foresight scenarios:
1. **`REFERENCE_2026`**: Baseline empirical evidence from Malaysian labor market surveys and ILO projections.
2. **`MODERATE_COPILOT`**: Assistive AI adoption expanding technical worker productivity and task augmentation.
3. **`FAST_AGENTIC`**: Accelerated adoption of autonomous multi-agent workflows driving profound task substitution and demand restructuring.

## 2. Strict M6 and M5 Isolation Invariants

A paramount architectural requirement of Stage 14A is maintaining absolute decoupling between foresight modeling and sustainability taxonomy:

```
+---------------------------------------+
|  M6 Future Workforce Scenario Engine  |  (Read-Only: Task automation, augmentation, resilience)
+-------------------+-------------------+
                    |
                    | (Scenario Foresight Parameters)
                    v
+---------------------------------------+
|  O6 Scenario Aggregation Layer        |  <-- Consumes without mutating either model
+-------------------+-------------------+
                    ^
                    | (Qualitative Alignment Mappings)
+-------------------+-------------------+
|  M5 SDG Impact Mapping Engine         |  (Read-Only: Official UN target definitions)
+---------------------------------------+
```

### Mandatory Invariant Rules:
1. **Zero M5 Mutation**: Scenario conditions **never** modify, re-weight, or reclassify M5 SDG mappings. A skill that aligns to Target 9.5 as ENABLING under `REFERENCE_2026` remains strictly ENABLING under `FAST_AGENTIC`.
2. **Zero M6 Mutation**: O6 consumes M6 scenario definitions and occupation profiles as read-only data. O6 never adjusts M6 baseline weights, transition matrices, or task vulnerability scores.
3. **Structured Assumption Labeling**: All scenario comparison outputs must carry the explicit disclaimer:
   > *"Scenario comparisons represent structured macroeconomic and technological foresight assumptions from M6. They do not alter underlying M5 qualitative target definitions or statutory alignments."*

## 3. Empirical Scenario Audit: Digital Services Cohort ($N = 16$)

To audit the behavior of `compare_scenarios()`, the Digital Services cohort was evaluated across all three scenarios:

| MASCO Occupation | Role Title | Reference 2026 Resilience | Moderate Copilot Augmentation | Fast Agentic Automation Risk | Aligned Core SDGs |
|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **2512** | Software Developers | High ($78.4$) | Very High ($88.2$) | Elevated ($62.5$) | SDG 8, 9 |
| **2511** | Systems Analysts | High ($74.2$) | Very High ($85.0$) | Moderate ($48.0$) | SDG 8, 9 |
| **2521** | Database Designers & Admins | Moderate ($68.0$) | High ($79.5$) | Elevated ($68.0$) | SDG 8, 9 |
| **2529** | AI & Data Science Specialists | Very High ($89.5$) | Exceptional ($94.0$) | Low ($32.0$) | SDG 8, 9, 12, 16 |
| **2519** | Cloud & Integration Specialists | High ($81.0$) | High ($86.0$) | Moderate ($45.0$) | SDG 8, 9 |
| **3514** | Web Technicians & Cloud Operators | Moderate ($58.0$) | Moderate ($65.0$) | Very High ($76.0$) | SDG 8, 9 |

### Foresight Insights Generated by O6:
1. **Reskilling Urgency Escalation**: Under `FAST_AGENTIC`, entry-level technical roles such as Web Technicians (3514) face severe automation risk ($76.0$). O6 highlights that transitioning these workers into AI Governance (SDG 16) or Software Engineering (SDG 8/9) is a high-priority risk-mitigation strategy.
2. **Stable Sustainability Anchor**: While task automation risk fluctuates from $32.0$ to $76.0$ across roles, the cohort's qualitative alignment to **SDG 8** and **SDG 9** remains invariant at $100.0\%$, demonstrating that the workforce's fundamental societal contribution (enabling industrial technological upgrading) persists even as operational toolsets automate.
