## 1. Governance Standard for Explainability

To prevent black-box opacity in competition and downstream production systems, every scenario output generated by M6 must provide explicit answers to the **Five Foundational Foresight Questions**:
1. **WHAT CHANGED?** (Outlook tier, numerical indices, and delta relative to Reference).
2. **WHY?** (Interaction between automation pressure, augmentation leverage, and demand resilience).
3. **WHICH BASELINE M2 EVIDENCE MATTERED?** (Empirical shortage surveys, wage context, ILO task exposure).
4. **WHICH SCENARIO ASSUMPTIONS CAUSED THE CHANGE?** (Explicit parameter IDs and qualitative assumptions).
5. **HOW UNCERTAIN IS IT?** (Sensitivity tier, parameter range spread, and evidence quality flags).

## 2. Case Study 1: Software Developers (MASCO 2512)

```
================================================================================
                    CASE STUDY: SOFTWARE DEVELOPERS (2512)
================================================================================
Baseline M2 Outlook: STRONGLY RISING
Moderate Copilot Outlook: TRANSFORMING (Reskilling Urgency: MODERATE)
Fast Agentic Outlook:     TRANSFORMING (Reskilling Urgency: HIGH)
================================================================================
```

### Forensic Breakdown Under `FAST_AGENTIC`:
- **1. WHAT CHANGED?**:
  - Outlook shifted from `STRONGLY RESILIENT` in Reference to **`TRANSFORMING`** under Fast Agentic.
  - Automation pressure increased from $8.0 \to 15.9$ ($\Delta = +7.9$).
  - Augmentation opportunity expanded dramatically from $54.0 \to 84.0$ ($\Delta = +30.0$).
  - Role transition pressure rose from $20.2 \to 29.3$ ($\Delta = +9.1$).
  - Reskilling urgency escalated from `MODERATE` to **`HIGH`**.
- **2. WHY?**:
  - Autonomous agents automate routine code generation, test authoring, and boilerplate drafting. However, the software developer's role is **amplified rather than replaced** because developers orchestrate these agents to design larger, more complex systems. Net resilience remains very high ($S_{\text{resilience}} = 78.2$), but the day-to-day workflow experiences major restructuring ($\text{task\_transformation} = 50.0$).
- **3. WHICH BASELINE M2 EVIDENCE MATTERED?**:
  - *Critical Shortage*: TalentCorp MyCOL identified MASCO 2512 as critical in 8/8 consecutive editions ($89.6$ shortage level).
  - *ILO 2025 Exposure*: ILO Working Paper 140 establishes that augmentation potential ($0.54$) significantly exceeds automation exposure ($0.08$).
- **4. WHICH SCENARIO ASSUMPTION CAUSED THE CHANGE?**:
  - `PARAM-AGE-REACH` (Agentic Task Reach = 80.0, Very High): Autonomous agents execute multi-step tool use and debugging chains.
  - `PARAM-AGE-AUG` (Augmentation Intensity = 85.0, Very High): Developers gain massive throughput leverage.
- **5. HOW UNCERTAIN IS IT?**:
  - *Sensitivity*: **`HIGHLY SENSITIVE`** (Lower bound: `RESILIENT`, Central: `TRANSFORMING`, Upper: `TRANSFORMING`).
  - *Transition Pressure Uncertainty Band*: `[24.8, 33.7]`.
  - *Quality Flags*: `MAJOR_GROUP_WAGE_CONTEXT`, `NO_ROLE_SPECIFIC_WAGE_DATA`.

### Forensic Breakdown Under `FAST_AGENTIC`:
- **1. WHAT CHANGED?**:
  - Outlook deteriorated from `PRESSURED` in Reference to **`HIGHLY PRESSURED`** under Fast Agentic.
  - Automation pressure surged from $25.0 \to 49.8$ ($\Delta = +24.8$).
  - Augmentation opportunity saw modest growth from $17.0 \to 26.5$ ($\Delta = +9.5$).
  - Role transition pressure climbed sharply from $35.5 \to 51.0$ ($\Delta = +15.5$).
  - Reskilling urgency reached **`HIGH`**.
- **2. WHY?**:
  - Executive administrative workflows (calendar scheduling, meeting minutes synthesis, travel booking, document formatting, email triage) are directly inside the core capabilities of autonomous agentic software. Because baseline employer shortage evidence is low ($20.0$), the role lacks the demand cushion to absorb task substitution.
- **3. WHICH BASELINE M2 EVIDENCE MATTERED?**:
  - *Surplus / Declining Demand*: TalentCorp MyCOL shows zero critical listings (surplus labour pool).
  - *ILO 2025 Benchmark*: High clerical cognitive automation exposure ($0.25$) with limited augmentation offset.
- **4. WHICH SCENARIO ASSUMPTION CAUSED THE CHANGE?**:
  - `PARAM-AGE-AUTO` (Automation Intensity = 85.0): Structured administrative workflows face aggressive end-to-end tool replacement.
- **5. HOW UNCERTAIN IS IT?**:
  - *Sensitivity*: **`ROBUST`** (Remains `HIGHLY PRESSURED` across lower, central, and upper parameter bounds).
  - *Transition Pressure Uncertainty Band*: `[44.2, 57.8]`.
  - *Quality Flags*: `MAJOR_GROUP_WAGE_CONTEXT`, `NO_ROLE_SPECIFIC_WAGE_DATA`.
