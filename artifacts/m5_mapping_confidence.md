## 1. Overview & Purpose

The **M5 Mapping Confidence Framework** provides an objective, reproducible grading mechanism for assessing the evidentiary certainty of skill and occupational SDG alignments. Rather than relying on subjective intuition, confidence levels are derived from multi-source corroboration, institutional publication authority, and directness of semantic connection to official UN indicators.

---

## 2. Confidence Level Hierarchy

| Confidence Level | Definition | Mandatory Evidentiary Requirements | System Action |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HIGH** | Explicit, authoritative, multi-source corroborated alignment. | 1. Direct correspondence with official UN target text or IAEG indicator metadata.<br>2. Corroborated across $\ge 2$ independent authoritative taxonomies or institutional reports (e.g. UNESCO, ILO, IPCC, MASCO 2020, Bursa Malaysia).<br>3. Zero counter-evidence in professional practice. | Eligible for primary display in learner pathways and employer reports. |
| **MEDIUM** | Plausible, context-dependent, or single-source corroborated alignment. | 1. Logical causal mechanism established.<br>2. Documented in at least 1 authoritative policy report or academic benchmark.<br>3. Contribution depends partially on deployment environment or enterprise sector. | Displayed with explanatory context or relegated to secondary alignments. |
| **LOW** | Speculative, indirect, or highly tenuous alignment. | 1. No direct policy citation.<br>2. Causal chain exceeds two steps.<br>3. High vulnerability to greenwashing accusations. | **PROHIBITED IN M5 ENGINE.** (Rejected during schema curation). |
