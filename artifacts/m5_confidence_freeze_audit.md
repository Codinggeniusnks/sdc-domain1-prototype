## 1. Critical Configuration Operator Audit (Check 32 Verification)

Stage 13D conducted a forensic inspection of `data/sdg/m5_config_v1_1.json` and the confidence rating engine to verify that no inverted or malformed operators exist:

### A. Nature of M5 Confidence Ratings:
- Confidence in M5 is a **qualitative categorical rating** (`HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`), not a continuous numeric probability generated by a machine learning score.
- Every alignment in `data/sdg/skill_sdg_mappings_v1_1.json` and `data/sdg/role_sdg_mappings_v1_1.json` explicitly assigns a validated confidence level based on deterministic qualitative criteria.

### B. Exact Qualitative Calibration Rules in `m5_config_v1_1.json`:
- **`HIGH`**: "Requires unambiguous semantic fit to target text, direct or foundational enabling causality, independent multi-source corroboration, and zero unstated sector dependencies."
- **`MEDIUM`**: "Applies to valid mappings that are sector-dependent (`SECTOR_DEPENDENT_ALIGNMENT`), application-dependent (`CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_ALIGNMENT`), or contextual beneficiary alignments (`CONTEXTUAL_BENEFICIARY_ALIGNMENT`)."
- **`LOW`**: "Permissible but weak causal linkage; eliminated from core candidate mappings to prevent noise."

### C. The 80% Constraint is an Aggregate Registry-Level Ceiling:
- Stage 13B review finding P2-3 established that the *proportion* of `HIGH` confidence ratings across the entire mapping registry must not exceed 80.0% to prevent overconfidence saturation.
- In `scripts/run_m5_benchmarks_v1_1.py` (Benchmark BM13) and `src/sdg_impact_engine.py` line 560:
  ```python
  high_pct = (high_count / total_count * 100) if total_count > 0 else 0
  assert 60.0 <= high_pct <= 80.0
  ```
- **Operator Verification**: The check tests whether `high_pct <= 80.0` at the dataset level. It is NOT an individual mapping numeric filter (`score <= 80`).
- **Conclusion**: There is **zero operator inversion** or malformed logic. The implementation is 100% correct.

## 3. Orthogonality of Confidence vs Alignment Strength (Check 36 Verification)

M5 strictly maintains `alignment_strength` (`STRONG`, `MODERATE`, `LIMITED`) and `confidence` (`HIGH`, `MEDIUM`) as orthogonal dimensions:
- `alignment_strength` measures the **functional proximity** between the skill/task and the target indicator.
- `confidence` measures the **evidentiary certainty and degree of unconditional validity**.

### Concrete Empirical Cases of `STRONG` + `MEDIUM`:
1. **MASCO 2151 (Electrical Engineers) $\to$ Target 7.1**:
   - `alignment_strength`: `STRONG` (Designing power transmission and distribution infrastructure directly delivers electrification).
   - `confidence`: `MEDIUM` (Because the alignment is contingent on deployment within clean grid / renewable electrification projects, carrying `SECTOR_DEPENDENT_ALIGNMENT`).
2. **MASCO 2143 (Environmental Engineers) $\to$ Target 13.2**:
   - `alignment_strength`: `STRONG` (Engineering industrial carbon capture and facility decarbonization directly integrates climate mitigation).
   - `confidence`: `MEDIUM` (Because generic environmental engineers may also work on non-climate municipal drainage permits, carrying `SECTOR_DEPENDENT_ALIGNMENT`).

## 4. Multi-Source Family Deduplication (Check 35 Verification)

- **`WRI_WBCSD`**: World Resources Institute / World Business Council for Sustainable Development (GHG Protocol).

- **`MALAYSIA_GOV_POLICY`**: Ministry of Economy / MITI (NETR, NIMP 2030, MASCO 2020).

Multiple citations from the same institution (e.g. two separate Bursa Malaysia guidelines) are treated as a single source family, ensuring rigorous corroboration.
