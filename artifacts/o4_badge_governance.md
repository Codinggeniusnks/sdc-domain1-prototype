# O4 Non-Competitive Badge Governance & Ethical Charter v1.0.0

## 1. Ethical Stance on Workplace Gamification
Conventional enterprise gamification often relies on competitive mechanisms—such as real-time leaderboards, percentile comparisons, and velocity competitions—which induce workplace stress, encourage superficial credential farming, and enable subtle workplace surveillance and discrimination.

The **O4 Engine** establishes an explicit ethical charter for recognition badges:

## 2. Hard Algorithmic Guardrails
1. **Zero Peer Ranking**: Badges must never compute, display, or store worker-against-worker rankings or percentiles.
2. **No Public Leaderboards**: The platform does not implement, expose, or export leaderboard endpoints.
3. **No Negative Badges or Social Shaming**: Badges are purely accretive; there are no penalty states, inactivity flags, or lag indicators.
4. **Intrinsic Purpose**: Badges reflect personal commitment and milestone achievement, encouraging adult learners to sustain 5–8 hours/week learning trajectories.

## 3. Approved Badge Taxonomy
