## 1. Naming Architecture

To maintain continuity with the competition brief and reflect the full 12-engine sovereign enterprise architecture, the naming is intentionally structured into two clear tiers:

### Primary System / Technical Architecture Name:
> **National AI Workforce Reskilling & Foresight Engine** (Version: `v1.0.0`)
- **Usage:** Official technical documentation, Technical Declaration form, system architecture diagrams, freeze manifests, API declarations, audit ledgers, and academic/institutional reporting.
- **Rationale:** Accurately reflects the comprehensive 12-module sovereign workforce intelligence system incorporating MASCO 2020 occupational taxonomy, longitudinal trend intelligence (M2 v1.1.1), macroeconomic scenario stress-testing (M6 v1.1.0), sequential reskilling pathways (M4 v1.1.0), causal UN SDG impact mapping (M5 v1.1.0 & O6 v1.1.0), enterprise workforce gap analytics (O1 v1.1.0), automated replanning (O2 v1.0.0), trilingual presentation (O3 v1.0.0), sovereign milestone tracking (O4 v1.0.0), and Responsible-AI governance (O5 v1.0.0).

### User-Facing Product Brand / Application Title:
> **AI Skills Compass**
- **Usage:** Header title in the web application UI, dashboard browser tab title, live judge walkthrough pitch, and learner-facing navigation cues.
- **Rationale:** Clean, memorable, and human-centric metaphor guiding Malaysian workers, graduates, and HR leaders from *"who I am today"* to *"where I can be in the AI era"* across sovereign reskilling pathways.
