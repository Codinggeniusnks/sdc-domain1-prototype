## 3. Flagged Document Contradictions (Require Manual Confirmation with Organisers)

> [!WARNING]
> Do not silently assume one rule over another where the official documents conflict. Confirm the following items on the official Discord (`https://discord.gg/p72amSbw7X`) or handle both safely as noted below:

1. **GitHub Repository Creation Timing**:
   - *Participant Handbook §6.1.2 (p. 9)* instructs teams to *"create and prepare their GitHub repository before the hacking period begins."*
   - *Terms & Conditions §5.5.2–5.5.3 (p. 20)* states *"The repository shall be created only after the official commencement of the Competition. Teams shall not recycle repositories that were created before the Competition unless prior written approval is obtained from the Organiser."* (Since T&C has legal precedence under §3 of both Handbooks, ensure the official team repo is created after 19 Oct 2026 8:00 PM MYT or confirm pre-created template forks on Discord).
2. **GitHub Repository Visibility (Private + Collaborators vs. Public)**:
   - *Participant Handbook §6.1.2–6.1.3 (p. 9) & §6.3.5.5 (p. 13)* instructs teams to set the repository to **private** during the hacking period and add the committee's designated GitHub account(s) as collaborators before the deadline.
   - *Participant Handbook §10.3 (p. 29)* states *"The submitted GitHub repository must be set to **public** and must remain accessible to the organizing committee and judging panel throughout the judging period."*
   - *Safe resolution*: Keep private during active hacking if desired, add any committee GitHub collaborator accounts announced on Discord, **and** switch to public immediately before submitting on `sdchack.my` (unless Discord instructs otherwise).
3. **RunPod Mandatory vs. Conditional GPU Usage**:
   - *GitHub Submission Template `README.md` (lines 151, 198)* labels `"How We Use RunPod (Required)"` and includes `SDC2026_TeamName_Runpod_POU.pdf` in the submission checklist.
   - *Participant Handbook §9.3–9.4 (pp. 28–29), §10.7 (p. 29), and FAQ 15.21 (p. 43)* explicitly states: *"Teams must use RunPod for **any work that needs a GPU**... Teams may also use any other tools, frameworks, or services for work that does not need a GPU"* and *"Teams that used RunPod must include a screenshot..."*
   - *Safe resolution*: Confirm on Discord whether non-GPU projects must still include a placeholder `SDC2026_TeamName_Runpod_POU.pdf` explaining why GPU was not required, or if a lightweight GPU embedding/reranking endpoint on RunPod Serverless is preferred by sponsors.
4. **Final Pitching Time & Venue on 1 November 2026**:
   - *Participant Handbook §6.7.1 (p. 20)* states final pitching is **6:00:00 PM to 8:30:00 PM MYT** in **A3#517 and A3#516**.
   - *Participant Handbook §7.2 Rundown (p. 26)* lists Parallel Track Pitching at **09:40–12:40** and **13:40–15:40**, with Closing Ceremony at **16:00–18:30**, and *§5 Venue Table (p. 7)* lists Pitching Rooms as **A4#113 and A5#119**.
5. **Replacement Finalist Confirmation Duration on 29 October 2026**:
   - *Terms & Conditions §8.2.6 (p. 33)* states replacement teams notified after 12:00 PM MYT have *"five (5) hours, until 29th October, 8:00 PM MYT"* (12:00 PM to 8:00 PM is 8 hours, not 5 hours).
6. **Submission Deadline Time of Day on 25 October 2026**:
   - *Submission Template Cover (p. 1)* says `Submission Deadline: 25 October 2026` without a timestamp, whereas *Participant Handbook §6.3.1 (p. 10) & §7.1 (p. 23)* specifies **25 October 2026, 7:59:59 AM MYT (UTC+8)** (morning, not midnight!). Treat **7:59:59 AM MYT** as the hard deadline.

## 4. Concise Prototype Acceptance Checklist

### A. Preliminary-Round Requirements (Must Be Working & Submitted by 25 Oct 2026, 07:59:59 AM MYT)
- [ ] **Synthetic Profile Intake**: Web form/selector with realistic sample profiles (no real personal data or real CVs) plus custom skill/interest entry.
- [ ] **Dated, Updatable AI/Market Signal Layer**: Live fetch from a verifiable public source (with publication date, retrieval timestamp, source URL, and extracted skills/roles) + clearly labeled dated snapshot fallback if offline.
- [ ] **Guardrail for Malformed/Empty Signals**: Validation check ensuring empty, stale, or malformed signal data returns `"insufficient evidence"` rather than fabricating confident recommendations.
- [ ] **Explainable Future-Role Matching**: Transparent, inspectable scoring formula showing matched profile skills, missing skill gaps, why the role ranks where it does, cited dated signals, and explicit uncertainty/limitations.
- [ ] **Ordered Reskilling Pathway**: Step-by-step learning sequence with estimated hours/weeks using verified free/low-cost or HRD Corp-aligned skill objectives (never inventing course names, prices, or guaranteed outcomes).
- [ ] **Meaningful SDG Mapping**: Every recommended role and skill mapped to specific UN SDGs and sub-targets (e.g., SDG 4.3, 4.4, 8.2, 8.5, plus domain-specific SDGs like 7, 9, 11, 13) explaining how the career path contributes.
- [ ] **Multi-Scenario Uncertainty Handling**: At least two distinct future scenarios (e.g., *Moderate Adoption / Co-Pilot Era* vs. *Fast Agentic Disruption*) that visibly change role rankings/urgency and state assumptions clearly.
- [ ] **Replit & GitHub Compliance**: Runs cleanly via `.replit` (`python src/main.py`), uses environment variables/Secrets (`.env.example`), includes Apache 2.0 `LICENSE`, unit tests (`python -m unittest discover tests`), and completed preliminary proposal + tech declaration text.

### B. Final-Round Ambitions (Phase 2 Upgrading & Physical Finale, 28 Oct – 1 Nov 2026)
- [ ] **Employer / HR Workforce View**: Team-level skills-gap heatmap and cohort reskilling planner across multiple synthetic employee profiles.
- [ ] **Automatic Re-Planning Alerts**: Diff detection showing how newly ingested market signals shift an existing user's role ranking and learning pathway.
- [ ] **Multilingual & Low-Bandwidth Polish**: Full toggle for English, Bahasa Malaysia, and Chinese UI labels.
- [ ] **Fairness & Bias Auditing**: Automated demographic-blindness check verifying identical skill inputs produce identical rankings regardless of name/age/background metadata.
- [ ] **Aggregated SDG Impact Dashboard**: Organization- or cohort-level rollup of SDG sub-target contributions.
