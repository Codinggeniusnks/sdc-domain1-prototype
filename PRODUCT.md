# PRODUCT.md — AI Skills Compass (SDC Hackathon 2026 · Domain 1)

## Product Purpose
AI Skills Compass is a human-centred workforce readiness and reskilling decision-support application built for the Malaysia-Japan-International SDC Hackathon 2026 (Domain 1: The Aiversecity Future-Ready Workforce Domain).

It bridges the gap between Malaysia's informal AI usage (56% of workers) and formal structured reskilling (26%) by connecting individual worker profiles and HR cohorts to dated scholarly research signals (OpenAlex CC0), transparent role-readiness scoring, low-cost/free learning pathways (AI Untuk Rakyat, HRD Corp e-LATiH), and UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 4 & SDG 8).

## Target Audience & Surface Mode
- **Surface Mode**: `Operate` (Task-driven diagnostic workbench & decision-support dashboard).
- **Primary Users**:
  1. **Malaysian Workers & Graduates (Individual View)**: Evaluating current skills against emerging AI roles, stress-testing career resilience across future disruption scenarios, and following an actionable, low-cost reskilling roadmap.
  2. **Employers, HR Leads & Policy Evaluators (Cohort View)**: Auditing department-wide skill coverage, identifying critical capability blind spots, and planning HRD Corp-claimable cohort training.
  3. **Hackathon Judges**: Inspecting epistemic honesty, deterministic scoring formulas, dated signal citations, and edge-case guardrails within seconds.

## Non-Negotiable Product Guardrails (Domain 1 Rule §6)
- **Public & Synthetic Data Only**: Zero real personal data (PII) or private CVs stored or transmitted.
- **Epistemic Honesty**: Signals represent dated scholarly AI research publications (OpenAlex / arXiv), never fabricated job vacancy counts or salary promises.
- **Human-in-Charge Advisory**: Every recommendation exposes its exact mathematical formula (`0.55 × Skill Readiness + 0.25 × Research Signal Coverage + 0.20 × Scenario Fit`), explicit uncertainty bands, and assumptions.
