## 1. Executive Summary & Foundational Axiom

The **O6 SDG Contribution Aggregation Engine v1.0.0** is an optional downstream analytics module designed to synthesize and summarize workforce sustainability capability and alignment information across three distinct operational tiers:
1. **Individual Level**: Profile-specific alignment mapping across confirmed skills, occupational roles, and reskilling pathways.
2. **Workforce / Cohort Level**: Privacy-preserving statistical aggregation across enterprise divisions or employer reskilling cohorts.
3. **Regional / Synthetic Population Level**: High-level macro capability tracking across geographic economic zones using non-identifying demo distributions.

### The Foundational Methodological Axiom
> **Core Principle**: O6 is strictly a **DESCRIPTIVE ALIGNMENT ANALYTICS ENGINE**, not an impact-measurement system.  
> It aggregates qualitative competency alignments anchored in the official UN SDG indicator framework via M5 v1.1.0. O6 does **NOT** measure, estimate, or claim to determine real-world societal impact, metric tons of carbon avoided, jobs generated, poverty alleviated, or national target achievement.

## 2. Approved vs. Prohibited Language & Conceptual Boundaries

To maintain rigorous scientific and legal integrity and prevent greenwashing, O6 enforces strict lexical and conceptual standards:

| Approved Descriptive Concept | Approved Terminology | Prohibited Formulation (Greenwashing / Unsubstantiated) |
|---|---|---|
| **Alignment Frequency** | *"SDG Alignment Prevalence ($SAP_s$)"*, *"Target Prevalence Index ($TPI_t$)"* | *"SDG impact achieved"*, *"Company contributes 35% to SDG 13"* |
| **Talent Capabilities** | *"Workforce Capability Representation"*, *"Skill Coverage Ratio"* | *"Workforce sustainability performance score"*, *"Green company index"* |
| **Evidence Tiers** | *"DIRECT / ENABLING / CONTEXTUAL Mapping Distribution"* | *"12 tonnes of CO2 saved"*, *"Carbon avoidance points"* |
| **Reskilling Horizon** | *"Projected Capability Alignment If Pathway Completed"* | *"Future impact generated by reskilling"*, *"Guaranteed sustainable transition"* |
| **Individual Context** | *"Profile aligned competencies"*, *"Target coverage"* | *"Personal SDG impact score"*, *"Employee sustainability rank"*, *"Green employee grade"* |

## 3. Multilevel Aggregation Architecture

O6 operates strictly bottom-up, maintaining complete independence from upstream recommendation scoring:

```
[Level 3: Regional Synthetic Population]  <-- Demographic-free demo zones (e.g. REGION_A, REGION_B)
                 ^
                 | Aggregated Cohorts (k >= 5)
[Level 2: Enterprise Workforce Cohort]   <-- Privacy-safe cohort statistics (e.g. DIGITAL_SERVICES)
                 ^
                 | Evaluated Profiles (Deduplicated)
[Level 1: Individual Profile Alignment]   <-- Current confirmed skills + MASCO role + pathway skills
                 ^
                 | Qualitative Lookups
[M5 Frozen Core: 16 Skills & 20 Roles]    <-- Target-grounded UN 2030 mappings
```

### 3.1 Level 1: Individual Alignment View
For a single worker profile, O6 retrieves the qualitative SDG mappings associated with their active occupational role and confirmed technical skills.
- **Deduplication Rule**: If an individual holds 4 distinct skills that all align to Target 9.5 (e.g., `python_scripting`, `sql_data_analysis`, `api_integration`, `workflow_automation`), Target 9.5 is recorded as represented **once** in the individual's target coverage list.
- **Caveat & Flag Preservation**: Individual profiles inherit all relevant quality flags from M5 (e.g., `SECTOR_DEPENDENT_ALIGNMENT`, `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT_ALIGNMENT`, `CONTEXTUAL_BENEFICIARY_ALIGNMENT`).
- **Zero Ranking**: Individual profiles receive descriptive counts only; no "sustainability score" or comparative ranking is ever calculated.

### 3.2 Level 2: Workforce / Company Cohort View
Aggregates a set of worker profiles belonging to an enterprise organization or training cohort:
- Evaluates total cohort size ($N$) and eligible deduplicated profiles ($N_{\text{elig}}$).
- Applies **Minimum Cohort Size Suppression** ($N_{\text{elig}} \ge 5$).
- Computes aggregate prevalence ratios, mapping type distributions, and capability coverage metrics.
- Exposes decoupled interface hooks for downstream modules (O1 gap analysis, O2 refresh, O5 fairness auditing).

### 3.3 Level 3: Regional / Synthetic Population View
Aggregates profiles across synthetic demo regions (e.g., `REGION_A`, `REGION_B`, `REGION_C`):
- Uses purely synthetic, non-identifying worker records.
- Provides macro-level capability benchmarks for regional talent development planning.
- Mandatory disclaimer: Results represent synthetic simulations and do not reflect official national census or state statistics.

## 4. Deduplication and Non-Double-Counting Principles

A fundamental requirement of Stage 14A is ensuring mathematical rigor and preventing artificial metric inflation:

1. **Profile-Level Deduplication**: Duplicate profile IDs in input batches are deterministically detected and deduplicated prior to denominator calculation. The raw cohort size and eligible count are both reported.
2. **Prevalence Denominator Invariance**: The denominator for cohort prevalence metrics is always the eligible cohort size ($N_{\text{elig}}$).
3. **Single Person per Goal ($SAP_s$)**: A worker holding multiple skills and an occupational role that all map to SDG 8 contributes exactly $+1$ to the numerator for SDG 8. The prevalence ratio $SAP_s \in [0.0, 1.0]$.
4. **Single Person per Target ($TPI_t$)**: A worker holding multiple skills mapping to Target 9.5 contributes exactly $+1$ to the numerator for Target 9.5. The prevalence ratio $TPI_t \in [0.0, 1.0]$.
5. **Mapping Distribution Accounting**: When evaluating the distribution of evidence tiers (DIRECT, ENABLING, CONTEXTUAL), O6 sums unique profile-target alignment instances across the cohort and reports normalized shares that sum strictly to $1.0$.

## 5. Temporal Capability Dimensions: CURRENT vs. POST_PATHWAY

O6 provides two distinct temporal analytical lenses:
1. **`CURRENT`**:
   - Evaluates confirmed, active skills and current occupational role.
   - Represents the verified current capability baseline of the individual or workforce.
2. **`POST_PATHWAY`**:
   - Evaluates confirmed skills **plus** planned skills from the M4 reskilling pathway, along with the target career role.
   - Represents a **PROJECTED CAPABILITY ALIGNMENT IF PATHWAY COMPLETED**.
   - Mandatory Guardrail: Must **never** be presented as guaranteed future impact, training completion, or verified competence.

---

## 6. Prohibited Synthetic Weighting and Scoring Schemes

To prevent the creation of pseudo-scientific impact metrics, O6 explicitly rejects arbitrary cardinal weighting:
- **No Mapping Type Points**: DIRECT, ENABLING, and CONTEXTUAL are **never** assigned points (e.g., $3, 2, 1$) and summed into a score.
- **No Strength Multipliers**: Alignment strengths (STRONG, MODERATE, LIMITED) remain purely categorical labels.
- **No Confidence Arithmetic**: Mapping confidence (HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW) is preserved categorically. O6 never multiplies confidence $\times$ strength to synthesize impact points.

---

## 7. Strict Governance & Downstream Safety

1. **HR Decision Prohibition**: O6 workforce analytics must **never** be used for hiring, firing, promotion, compensation, disciplinary action, or performance scoring.
2. **Upstream Isolation**: O6 operates strictly read-only. Aggregation outputs never alter M3 role matching scores, M4 course selection, or M5 mapping rules.
3. **Privacy by Design**: Mandatory minimum cohort size ($k \ge 5$) prevents inferential disclosure of individual competencies.
